About

I am a Data Scientist at Phoenix Financial and a PhD researcher in Data Science at Tel Aviv University. I hold an MSc in Statistics and undergraduate degrees in Statistics & Data Science and Economics from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem.

My industry experience includes predictive modeling, statistical analysis, forecasting, and AI-driven data processing. I work on translating complex data and business problems into statistical and machine learning solutions, alongside colleagues in data engineering and ML production.

My research explores how statistical structure and probabilistic representations can contribute to machine learning and generative modeling, bridging classical statistical inference and modern learning architectures.

Research & Publications

Probabilistic Multilabel Graphical Modelling of Motif Transformations in Symbolic Music

Ron Taieb, Yoel Greenberg, Barak Sober

A probabilistic graphical modeling framework for studying motif transformations in symbolic music, using multilabel conditional random fields and statistical inference.

Ongoing PhD Research — Statistical Representations for Generative Modeling

Investigating how statistical representations can be integrated into probabilistic generative models to capture structure in complex data. Current work explores conditional flow matching, state-space methods, and learned statistical representations for time-series generation.
