Ron Taieb

I am a Ph.D. student in Data Science at Tel Aviv University, working on probabilistic generative modeling, statistical machine learning, and time-series dynamics. My work is centered on building statistically grounded machine learning methods for structured data, with an emphasis on generative models, state-space representations, graphical models, and signal processing.

Alongside my academic work, I have industry and research experience in statistical modeling, forecasting systems, applied AI, document understanding, retrieval-augmented generation, and data infrastructure. I am especially interested in the intersection between rigorous statistical modeling and practical machine learning systems.

Projects

Beethoven Motif Transformations CRF

Probabilistic modeling of musical motif transformations in symbolic music using multilabel graphical models and conditional random fields. The project combines statistical modeling, symbolic music analysis, and interpretable structure learning.

[GitHub]

Diffusion Models with Alternative Noising Distributions and Schedules

A generative modeling project studying how alternative noising processes and scheduling choices affect diffusion-based learning and sampling behavior.

[GitHub]

Goodreads Fantasy Subgenre Classification

NLP and topic-modeling pipeline for classifying fantasy subgenres from large-scale book review data, combining language representation methods with interpretable unsupervised modeling.

[GitHub]

Robust K-Centroids Clustering

Comparative implementation and evaluation of K-centroids clustering algorithms, focusing on robustness, optimization, and empirical behavior across clustering settings.
